## 6. App Review Notes (İngilizce — kopyala/yapıştır)

```
ABOUT THE APP

Holavon is a one-to-one written consultation service, not a horoscope app or a
content aggregator. A single, identity-verified practitioner (Fatih Asartepe)
answers one question per purchase. This build contains only the consultation
workflow:

  1) sign in
  2) expert profile, method and service commitment
  3) sample consultation report (so the reviewer can see the deliverable)
  4) question form (topic, question text, birth date/time/place)
  5) one-time purchase of a consumable item ("sor_tek")
  6) written answer + downloadable PDF in "My questions"

The App Store build intentionally excludes daily horoscopes, sky calendars,
birth-chart generation or archives, synastry, chart tools, a PDF store, and the
social feed. Those features exist only in our separate Android product, which is
reviewed under a different submission.

WHY THIS IS NOT A DUPLICATE / TEMPLATE APP (Guideline 4.3)

- The product is a paid human service, not generated content: every answer is
  written by the named practitioner for that specific question.
- Service levels are contractual and visible in the app: written answer within
  24 hours, automatic refund if the answer does not arrive.
- The deliverable is a concrete artifact: a 3–5 page PDF consultation file that
  stays in the user's account.
- The app serves Turkish, English and Spanish customers.

HOW TO REVIEW

1. Launch the app and pick a language.
2. Sign in with the demo account below (the app is account-based):
     email: <DEMO_EMAIL>
     password: <DEMO_PASSWORD>
3. Tap "Sample report" to see the deliverable, and "Meet the expert" for the
   practitioner's profile and method.
4. Tap "Ask your question", fill in the form and proceed to the purchase screen.
   The consumable IAP is "sor_tek" (2,399.99 TRY). Use a sandbox Apple ID to
   complete it.
5. After purchase the question appears in "My questions" with status
   "Awaiting answer (24h)". Because a human answers, the written response and
   PDF are not instant. A pre-answered demo question is already present in the
   demo account so delivery, the written answer and the PDF can be reviewed
   without waiting.
6. "Account" → language, theme, sign out, delete account, privacy/terms/support.

PURCHASE AND REFUNDS

- Product type: Consumable, product ID "sor_tek". It is a single consultation,
  not a subscription; nothing renews.
- If no answer is delivered within 24 hours, the purchase is refunded
  automatically by our backend; users can also contact support at
  https://holavon.com/destek.

ACCOUNT DELETION (5.1.1(v))

Account deletion is in the app: Account → "Delete my account". It permanently
removes the account, the questions and the consultation files.

SIGN-IN

Only email/password sign-in is offered in this build; third-party social login is
disabled. (Google/Facebook sign-in exists only in the Android product.)

CONTACT

<SUPPORT_EMAIL> — we answer review questions within one business day.
```
